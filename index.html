For indie authors who run newsletter swaps. If you trade promotions with other authors through BookClicker and send with MailerLite, SwapWright does the repetitive part: finding partners, answering their offers, building your newsletter draft and telling every author their book went out.

The four jobs it does

Outreach

Works through the lists you have liked on BookClicker, judges which are worth a feature and which a mention, and sends each one an offer — rotating through your books so no title is pushed at every partner.

Replies

Answers the offers that come back, accepting the strongest and declining the ones that would waste your best slot, with the numbers behind every decision.

Newsletter

Builds the draft for a fully booked date — every cover, link, title and blurb, placed in your own template. Then it reopens the saved draft and checks it against what is booked.

Follow-ups

Emails every author you promoted with a link to the newsletter their book appeared in.

It never sends your newsletter. It writes the draft, unscheduled, into your MailerLite drafts. Reading it and pressing send stays yours.

Authors who already swap newsletter promotions with other authors and want the admin to stop eating an evening a week. It suits you if you have your own mailing list, you book swaps through BookClicker, and you send your newsletter with MailerLite. It is not for you if you do not run swaps, if your list lives somewhere other than MailerLite, or if you are on an Intel Mac.

How you get it

  1. Start the trial. You give card details but are not charged. Polar handles the payment and emails your receipt.
  2. Open the Access purchase link in your receipt. Your license key and the download are both on that page. The key is not written in the email itself, so there is no point searching it.

  4. Open it. It just opens. SwapWright is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS does not warn about it and there is nothing to approve.

  6. Sign in to BookClicker and MailerLite. Two ways, and the second needs no permissions at all:

Quit Google Chrome first if you are asked to — Chrome locks its own profile while it is open.
  7. Finish Setup: like the lists you want to swap with, add your book titles, and let it read your newsletter.

If something goes wrong

Find the words you are seeing. If none of these match, write to supportswapwright@gmail.com with the exact message or a screenshot, and say which step you had reached — that is usually enough to answer it in one reply.

It will not install, or nothing happens when you open it

  • “SwapWright is damaged and can’t be opened” — you have an old or partly downloaded file. Download it again from the Access purchase link in your receipt. The current version opens with no warning at all; if you get one, the file is wrong, not your Mac.
  • It bounces in the Dock, or says the application cannot be opened — check Apple menu → About This Mac. SwapWright needs Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and cannot run on an Intel Mac at all. If yours is Intel, write to us and we will refund you.

The license key

  • You cannot find a key in your receipt — it is not in the email. Open the Access purchase link; the key is on that page, with the download.
  • You cannot paste into the key field — right-click the field and choose Paste, or use ⌘V. On older versions right-click did nothing; the menu bar’s Edit → Paste works there.
  • “Activation limit reached” — your license runs on up to three computers. Press Release this computer in Setup on a Mac you still have, or write to us and we will free a slot.

Signing in to BookClicker or MailerLite

  • It says you are not signed in, or sends you back to a login page — use “Sign in here” in Setup. It signs you in inside the app and needs no permission from macOS.
  • “Chrome profile data not found” — despite the wording, this usually means macOS is blocking the app rather than that anything is missing. Grant Full Disk Access (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access → switch on SwapWright, then reopen it), or use “Sign in here” instead and skip the permission entirely.
  • It signs in as somebody else, or a name you do not recognize — it is using the wrong Chrome profile. Choose the right one in Setup, or use “Sign in here” to sign in directly and leave Chrome out of it.
  • “Quit Google Chrome first” — Chrome keeps an exclusive lock on its own profile. Quit it completely with ⌘Q and try again.
  • It worked before and stopped after an update — macOS ties Full Disk Access to each version of an app, so a new version arrives without the permission the last one had. Grant it again, or use “Sign in here”.

Still stuck

Nothing here needs you to reinstall, and nothing you do in Setup can lose your work: your settings, sign-ins and run history stay on your computer. If you would rather not troubleshoot at all, cancel inside the trial and you are charged nothing.

What it does not do

  • It does not send newsletters. Only you do that.
  • It does not touch a newsletter already scheduled in your outbox.
  • It does not buy paid placements. Free swaps only.
  • It does not send anything to us. Your settings, sign-ins and history stay on your computer — there is no SwapWright server and no tracking.
