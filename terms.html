License and payment

SwapWright is a monthly subscription with a 14-day free trial. You are not charged until the trial ends, and cancelling before then costs nothing.

A license is for one person and one computer. Moving to another machine is done from Setup — press “Release this computer” first, then activate on the new one. Do not share keys.

After the trial it renews monthly, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.

The app checks the license with Polar about twice a day. If it cannot reach them — you are offline, or they are down — it keeps working for seven days before asking you to sign in again, so a dropped connection never stops a run.

Payments are handled by Polar, who are the merchant of record. Their terms apply to every purchase, and ours add to them rather than replace them.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time from the link in your receipt. You do not need to ask us, and there is nothing to cancel inside the app itself.

Cancelling stops the next payment. It does not end the period you have already paid for — the app keeps working until that period runs out, and the automations stop then. Cancelling during the free trial means you are never charged at all.

Your settings, sign-ins and run history are on your own computer and stay there either way. Nothing is deleted when a subscription ends, and nothing is sent to us. Subscribing again later picks up where you left off.

Cancelling is not the same as a refund. It stops money going out in future; it does not return money already taken. For that, see below.

The trial is the time to decide. Once a payment has been taken it is not refunded: cancelling stops the next one and the app keeps working to the end of the period you have paid for.

Polar are the merchant of record for every purchase, and their own refund terms apply regardless of what is written here.
