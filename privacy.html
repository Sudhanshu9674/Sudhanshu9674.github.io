Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-27

SwapWright runs entirely on your own computer. It is not a service that holds your data — there is no SwapWright server, no account with us, and nothing about your books, your subscribers or your swap partners is sent anywhere by us.

Everything stays on your computer

Your settings, run history, logs and the signed-in browser profile are stored in a folder on your Mac (Library → Application Support → SwapWright). Nothing is uploaded.

We cannot see your newsletters, your subscriber list, your bookings, or which authors you swap with. None of it reaches us.

Your BookClicker and MailerLite sign-ins

SwapWright never asks for, stores, or transmits your passwords. You sign in yourself, either in a browser window the app opens or in Chrome, and the app reuses that session the way a browser tab does.

To avoid making you sign in before every run, the app saves that session — the browser cookies, not your password — in a file inside its own folder, readable only by your user account. It never leaves your computer. Signing out on the site, or deleting the SwapWright folder, ends it.

Setup no longer offers to read your Chrome profile — you sign in in the app’s own window. If your settings still name a Chrome profile from an earlier version, that is honoured: the app reads the session cookies for bookclicker.com and mailerlite.com from Chrome on your machine, so it can act as you on those two sites. Those cookies are used locally and are not sent anywhere else.

Sites the app contacts on your behalf

While running, the app visits bookclicker.com and mailerlite.com as you, and fetches book covers and descriptions from Amazon and BookFunnel. Those visits are subject to each site’s own privacy policy.

If your copy is licensed, the licence key is checked with Lemon Squeezy. That exchange carries the key and a name for this machine, nothing else.

No tracking

There is no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. The app does not phone home, and we have no way of knowing whether or how you use it.

Removing your data

Delete the SwapWright folder in Library → Application Support and drag the app to the Bin. That removes everything the app has stored, including the signed-in session.
